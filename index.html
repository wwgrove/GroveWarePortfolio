    About Me

    Who I am:

    I’m an industrial design student at Carleton University with a focus on footwear, detailed digital illustration, and CAD visualization.

    Where I go to school:

    Carleton University, School of Industrial Design.

    What I do here:

    I develop concepts from early sketches into considered product forms, precise vector drawings, polished renders, and physical prototypes. My work connects visual craft with practical design development, from fine linework through final presentation.

    My Tools of Choice:

    Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop, SolidWorks, Adobe XD, Vizcom, & Bambu Studio (FDM).
